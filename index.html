Official league rules · 2026

Unify Ballers League · Yahoo league #523612 · 12 teams · head-to-head · 0.5 PPR · no divisions.

Roster: QB, 2 WR, 2 RB, TE, W/R/T, K, DEF, 6 BN, 2 IR. No acquisition or trade count limits.

Waivers: FAB, continual rolling-list tiebreak; one-day waiver period; game time through Tuesday. Trades: league votes, two-day review; deadline November 28, 2026.

Playoffs: Six teams, Weeks 15–17; top two seeds receive quarterfinal byes. Reseeding enabled. Playoff matchup tie goes to team with better regular-season record against that opponent.

Regular-season standings tiebreak: winning percentage, then total fantasy points for wild-card ties, then points in the most recent completed matchup, working backward. No divisions.

Offense scoring

Passing: 1 point per 25 yards; 4 per pass TD; -1 interception; -1 pick-six thrown; +10 at 400 passing yards and +10 at 500 passing yards. Rushing: 1 per 10 yards; 6 per TD; +10 at 200 rushing yards. Receiving: 0.5 PPR; 1 per 10 yards; 6 per TD; +5 at 200 receiving yards and +10 at 300 receiving yards. Return TD 6; two-point conversion 2; fumble lost -2; offensive fumble-return TD 6.

Kicking

Field goals: 0–39 yards = 3, 40–49 = 4, 50+ = 5. PAT made = 1.

Defense / special teams

Sack 1; INT 2; fumble recovery 2; safety 2; blocked kick 2; touchdown 6; return touchdown 6; fourth-down stop 1; extra-point return 2. Points allowed: 0 = 10; 1–6 = 7; 7–13 = 4; 14–20 = 1; 21–27 = 0; 28–34 = -1; 35+ = -4.

About MIKEJ. GM

MIKEJ. GM is an independent, non-commercial, single-user project for fantasy football decision support. It aims to retrieve authorized read-only Yahoo Fantasy Sports data about league rules, rosters, matchups, player availability and transactions, where available.

There is no Yahoo API connection in this demo. No Yahoo credentials, private league data, or API tokens are collected or stored by this site. Review drafts are stored locally in your browser and can be cleared.

All recommendations are reviewed by the manager. Yahoo roster changes and transactions must be performed manually in Yahoo Fantasy Sports. MIKEJ. GM is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Yahoo.
